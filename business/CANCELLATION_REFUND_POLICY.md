# Founding Cancellation and Refund Policy

This is the proposed founding-customer policy. Charging remains disabled until
the owner approves the policy and completes provider test mode.

- Founding membership is billed monthly at the approved checkout price.
- A customer may cancel at any time before the next renewal date.
- Cancellation stops future renewals and service continues through the already-paid period.
- A customer may request a refund when the first paid brief is not delivered within seven days of its scheduled Monday, or when a delivered brief contains a material factual error that makes it unusable. The request must be made within seven days of delivery.
- An accurate brief that was delivered on time is not refundable solely because the customer chose not to use its recommendations or because it did not produce sales.
- Refunds required by applicable law take priority over this policy.
- No refund is issued automatically; the owner must review the evidence and approve it.
