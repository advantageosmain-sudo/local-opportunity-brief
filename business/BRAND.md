# Local Opportunity Brief — Brand System

## Position

Local Opportunity Brief is the calm Monday-morning planning desk for an
independent local retailer. It notices what is happening nearby, verifies the
facts, and turns the best opportunities into simple actions.

## Customer

The first customer is an owner-operated boutique, gift shop, or local apparel
store in the United States. The owner has limited planning time, relies partly
on local foot traffic, and wants useful marketing direction without giving
another company access to their accounts.

## Voice

- Clear
- Neighborly
- Evidence-aware
- Practical
- Calm
- Never patronizing

## Visual direction

The design combines a trustworthy community newspaper with a calm modern
business service.

- Cream paper background
- Deep evergreen ink
- Warm amber opportunity highlights
- Brick-red emphasis
- Georgia-style editorial headings
- Clean sans-serif instructions
- No generic technology gradients
- No fake customer photographs or invented testimonials

## Locked phrases

Primary headline:

> Know what's happening. Know what to do next.

Product explanation:

> Five verified local sales opportunities and a ready-to-use weekly action plan.

## Language rules

Say:

- verified source
- recommended action
- opportunity
- evidence
- customer fit
- prepare
- consider

Do not say:

- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed traffic
- effortless money
- secret hack
- viral
- foolproof
- fully passive
