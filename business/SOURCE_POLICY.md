# Approved Source and Evidence Policy

## Allowed sources

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Official city, county, school, college, library, tourism, chamber, market, or
   event websites
2. Official event organizer pages
3. Official public weather services
4. Customer-provided first-party information
5. Reputable local reporting used only as supporting evidence

## Required evidence fields

Every factual opportunity must include:

- Source name
- Exact source URL
- Event or opportunity date
- Location
- Date the source was checked
- Confidence rating
- Explanation of why it fits the customer

## Rejection rules

Reject a finding when:

- The event date is in the past.
- The source cannot be opened.
- The location is outside the customer's approved radius.
- Two sources materially disagree and the official source cannot resolve it.
- The recommendation requires inventory the customer has not confirmed.
- The finding is copied from a competitor.
- The subject appears on the customer's exclusion list.
- The source prohibits the intended automated use.

## Demo-data rule

Fixture and sample sources must be visibly labeled as demonstrations. They must
never be delivered as live research.
