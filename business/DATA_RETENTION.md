# Data Retention and Privacy Requests

This is the private-pilot operating policy. It must receive owner and qualified
legal review before public launch.

## Retention schedule

- Founding requests that are not accepted: delete within 90 days of the final decision.
- Active customer profiles and weekly reports: retain while service is active and for up to 12 months after cancellation.
- Delivery receipts and payment-event records: retain for up to 24 months, or longer only when required for tax, fraud, dispute, or legal records.
- Failed or uncertain delivery diagnostics: retain until resolved, then delete within 90 days.
- Demo and owner-only pilot packets: retain only while needed for product validation.

## Customer requests

Customers may request a copy, correction, or deletion of their information by
replying to a service email. Identity must be verified before disclosing or
deleting records. A deletion request does not override records that must be kept
for a legal, tax, fraud, or payment dispute reason.

## Data minimization

Do not collect card details, bank information, customer lists, social media
passwords, or storefront credentials. Payment details remain with the approved
payment provider.
