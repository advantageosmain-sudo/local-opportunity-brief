# Pricing Decision

## Founding membership

- Price: **$49 per month**
- Capacity: first **10** accepted businesses
- Delivery: one brief each Monday
- Contents: five verified opportunities, campaign actions, captions, one
  customer email, a 30-day watchlist, and original source links
- Cancellation: any time before the next renewal

Submitting a membership request does not create a charge. Each applicant is
reviewed for source coverage before receiving a checkout link.

## Regular-price hypothesis

The current regular-price hypothesis is **$79 per month**. It is not approved
for use until the founding validation shows:

1. At least five businesses complete the trial.
2. At least three pay for a second month.
3. Average opportunity relevance is at least 4 out of 5.
4. Research and delivery cost remains below 20 percent of revenue.
5. Owner review averages less than ten minutes per report.

## Refund handling

No automatic refund action is authorized. A refund request must create an owner
decision record before any payment action is taken.
