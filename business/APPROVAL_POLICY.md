# Owner Approval Policy

## Owner approval is required before

- Sending a live customer report
- Activating live outbound email
- Charging or refunding a customer
- Changing pricing
- Publishing the service publicly
- Adding a new customer category
- Buying a dataset, service, advertisement, domain, or subscription
- Making a guarantee or legal promise

## Report approval packet

An approval packet must show:

- Customer and report week
- All five opportunities
- Exact dates and locations
- Evidence links
- Confidence scores
- Exact offer wording
- Exact social and email wording
- Validation result
- Known warnings
- Delivery recipient

## Safe default

Missing, unclear, or expired approval means **do not send**.

The delivery program also requires `LIVE_DELIVERY=1` and a configured email
provider. Approval alone cannot accidentally activate delivery.
